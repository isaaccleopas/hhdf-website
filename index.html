About us

Committed to the Body of Christ and the communities we serve.

Hope Healing and Development Foundation (HHDF) is a registered faith-based, non-profit organization based in Gombe State, Nigeria (CAC No: RC 9492944; SCUML: 161401281).

We are committed to bringing hope, healing, and transformation to individuals and communities through the love of Jesus Christ. HHDF exists to serve by addressing spiritual, emotional, and social needs, partnering with churches, ministries, and organizations to advance the Kingdom of God.

Our work focuses on making God’s Word accessible, bringing healing to broken lives, strengthening disciples of Christ, and empowering the next generation through holistic ministry.

Vision

Transformed lives, restored communities, and the Gospel reaching every people group.

We envision a world where individuals experience true hope and healing through the Word of God, where every community has access to Scripture in their heart language, and where the Body of Christ works together in unity beyond denominational boundaries— especially among the unreached and underserved.

Mission

Share the Gospel and demonstrate Christ’s love through holistic programs.

We advance Bible Translation and Scripture Engagement, Trauma Healing and Emotional Support, Discipleship, Evangelism and Church Planting, and Youth and Children development—equipping leaders who will impact their generation for Christ.

Key programs

Holistic ministry that meets spiritual, emotional, and social needs.

01

Bible Translation and Scripture Engagement

Making God’s Word accessible through Oral Bible Translation, Written Bible Translation, and Scripture Engagement—helping communities receive, understand, and apply Scripture in their heart languages.

02

Trauma Healing and Emotional Support

Providing biblical trauma healing and emotional support to individuals and communities experiencing pain, grief, suffering, and difficult life experiences—bringing restoration through the love of Jesus Christ.

03

Discipleship, Evangelism, and Church Planting

Making disciples, sharing the Gospel, strengthening believers, and supporting church planting—raising faithful followers of Jesus Christ across communities.

04

Youth and Children Development Initiatives

Investing in the next generation through mentoring, leadership development, and children support—empowering young people and vulnerable children to grow spiritually, socially, and practically.
